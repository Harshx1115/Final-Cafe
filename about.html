ABOUT US

our story

Brewed in Manchester, NH

Brew & Co was born in the heart of UNH in Manchester, New Hampshire. A city where small businesses still feel like home and a great cup of coffee is a reason to slow down. We opened our doors online through a simple website project.

What We Stand For

01

Locally Sourced

We work with New England farms and roasters whenever we can, because great coffee starts with great ingredients.

02

Hand-Crafted

Every drink is pulled, poured, and finished by hand. No shortcuts, no compromises, just care in every cup.

03

Community First

More than just a cafe, we're a gathering place for Manchester. Come for the coffee, stay for the conversation.
